## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an uncompromising philosophy of restraint, optical clarity, and tactile digital craftsmanship. It pairs expansive negative space with surgical typographic hierarchy to produce a sensation of effortless utility, quiet luxury, and industrial-grade refinement.

### Aesthetic Principles
- **Atmospheric Clarity:** Pure white canvases layered against soft warm-gray fields create a sense of breathing room, removing structural noise so content commands complete focus.
- **Micro-Deliberation:** Every hairline divider, radius transition, and subtle elevation layer is calibrated to feel milled rather than assembled.
- **Physicality via Optics:** Translucent frosted materials, delicate specular border reflections, and expansive low-contrast shadows recreate the optical behavior of precision-machined glass and ceramic hardware.
- **Commanding Typography:** Dramatic contrast between high-impact, tight-tracked display titles and crisp, utilitarian body copy establishes instant visual rhythm without ornamental clutter.

## Colors

The color architecture is rooted in high-fidelity neutral surfaces punctuated by an iconic, vivid cobalt accent. The palette avoids muddy midtones, favoring pure architectural whites, precise warm-gray structural surfaces, and deep typographic tones.

### Palette Application
- **Primary Canvas (`#FFFFFF`):** The baseline default for standard pages, modal backdrops, and active content panels.
- **Secondary Canvas (`#F5F5F7`):** Warm studio gray used for surrounding layouts, secondary groupings, cards, and inset surface trays.
- **Tertiary Surface (`#FBFBFD`):** Elevated optical bridge layer for floating toolbars, subtle hero callouts, and header navigation.
- **Primary Accent (`#0071E3`):** High-visibility dynamic blue reserved strictly for high-priority interactive affordances: primary CTA buttons, active state indicators, focused text carets, and key links.
- **Primary Ink (`#1D1D1F`):** Deep near-black providing maximum contrast without the harsh optical fatigue of absolute `#000000`.
- **Secondary Ink (`#6E6E73`):** Neutral gray calibrated to pass WCAG AAA legibility for supporting context, captions, and secondary metadata.
- **Hairline Dividing Tone (`#E5E5EA`):** Ultra-subtle boundary tone for 0.5px and 1px borders, separators, and non-intrusive containment rings.

## Typography

The typographic system relies on meticulous tracking adjustments, systematic optical sizing, and high contrast weights.

### Typographic Principles
- **Tracking Tightness:** Large scale display titles apply tight negative letter spacing (`-0.035em` to `-0.02em`) to mimic display-grade mechanical setting and maintain cohesion at scale.
- **Body Rhythm:** `body-lg` (17px) functions as the primary reading size for descriptive product overviews, ensuring optimal cadence and scannability against open layouts.
- **Uppercase Labels:** Micro labels and metadata captions employ slight positive tracking (`+0.04em`) with semi-bold weights to guarantee legibility at small scale.
- **Font Pairing Context:** The system uses Inter throughout for its neo-grotesque precision, uniform geometry, and extensive range of optical balance.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout model is governed by expansive vertical spacing coupled with strict horizontal alignment, using a 12-column responsive grid framed by generous gutter reserves.

### Grid Architecture
- **Desktop (>= 1024px):** 12 columns, `gutter-desktop` (32px), with container width capped at `max-width-container` (1152px) for structured application interfaces, expanding to 1440px for editorial showcases. Outer page padding is `space-3xl`.
- **Tablet (768px – 1023px):** 8 columns, `gutter-tablet` (24px), outer margins of `space-2xl` (48px). Multi-column product comparison grids collapse from 4 to 2 items per row.
- **Mobile (<= 767px):** 4 columns, `gutter-mobile` (16px), outer margins of `space-md` (16px). Grids collapse into vertical stacks with fluid horizontal carousel tracks for card groupings.

### Spacing Cadence
- Sections use deliberate breathing room (`space-3xl` to `space-section`) to visually isolate independent product narratives and technical details without explicit dividing panels.
- Micro-spacing (`space-xxs` to `space-sm`) maintains tight relationship coupling between labels, values, and contextual icons.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy is communicated through subtle layer separation and atmospheric lighting rather than heavy drop shadows or hard borders.

### Depth Hierarchy
1. **Base Tier (Ground):** The raw workspace canvas (`#FFFFFF` or `#F5F5F7`). Flat with no cast shadow.
2. **Subtle Inset / Card Tier:** Raised over the background with a 1px solid hairline (`#E5E5EA`) and an ultra-diffused micro shadow: `0 2px 8px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.04)`.
3. **Floating Overlays & Menus:** Uses high-refraction glassmorphism: `backdrop-filter: blur(20px) saturate(180%)`, combined with `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.8)` background fill, an inner perimeter line of `rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.6)`, and ambient shadow `0 12px 32px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`.
4. **Interactive Overlays & Modals:** Central focus sheets feature deep ambient dispersion: `0 24px 64px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.12)`, supported by a `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.2)` backdrop scrim.

## Shapes

The design system incorporates continuous squircle-inspired curvatures across all modules, prioritizing smooth corner transitions over mechanical, sharp edges.

### Geometric Rules
- **Base Components (Rounded Level 2):** Primary cards and containers use 16px (`1rem`) corner radii, escalating to 24px (`1.5rem`) on hero showcases.
- **Interactive Controls:** Form elements, segment switchers, and action chips adopt 10px to 12px radii.
- **Buttons & Pills:** High-action triggers, status tags, and floating filter nodes utilize fully rounded pill silhouettes (`border-radius: 9999px`) to create organic, touchable affordances.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Full pill profile (`rounded-full`), filled with `#0071E3`, text rendered in pure white `#FFFFFF` (`label-md`). Hover transitions smoothly to `#0077ED` with a delicate transform scale of `0.99` on press.
- **Secondary / Ghost:** Clear or `#F5F5F7` fill, 1px hairline perimeter (`#E5E5EA`), primary ink text (`#1D1D1F`). Hover triggers `#E8E8ED`.
- **Inline Text Link:** `#0071E3` with an integrated micro trailing chevron (`›`) that translates 2px horizontally on hover.

### Chips & Segmented Controls
- **Segmented Control:** Contained within a rounded track (`#E5E5EA` at 60% opacity or `#EFEFF4`). Active item rests on a white pill card elevated by `0 2px 6px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.08)`.
- **Filter Chip:** Subtle rounded pill (`rounded-full`), `#F5F5F7` background, `#1D1D1F` text. Active state transitions to solid `#1D1D1F` with white typography.

### Form Inputs & Checkboxes
- **Input Fields:** Crisp, minimal container with `#FFFFFF` background, 1px `#E5E5EA` border, and 12px radius. Focused state applies a strict 1px stroke of `#0071E3` with an outer glow of `0 0 0 4px rgba(0, 113, 227, 0.15)`. Labels sit above inputs in `#6E6E73` at `label-sm`.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** 18px diameter controls. Checked states fill with `#0071E3` accompanied by a pure white iconography checkmark. Unchecked state presents a clean 1.5px boundary in `#C7C7CC`.

### Cards & Feature Containers
- **Product Showcase Card:** Built on `#F5F5F7` or `#FFFFFF` with 20px radius, wrapped in a 1px border (`#E5E5EA`). Features balanced padding (`space-xl`), generous whitespace around assets, and a distinct top-aligned typographic group pairing `headline-md` with `body-md` in secondary ink.
- **Interactive Floating Tile:** Background utilizes frosted glass (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.75)` with blur). Scales up `1.01x` on hover with shadow deepening to `0 8px 24px rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.06)`.

### Lists
- Borderless rows separated exclusively by 0.5px hairline divider lines inset to align with the primary text column. Each row exhibits `#F5F5F7` wash on pointer hover.
