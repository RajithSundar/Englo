# Project Workspace Directives

## Design & UI/UX Standards
All frontend, interface, and design implementations must strictly adhere to the design rules in [.agent/rules/design-system.md](file:///d:/projects/Englo/.agent/rules/design-system.md):
- **Typography**: Choose intentional fonts (Inter, Plus Jakarta Sans, Geist), avoid default system fonts.
- **Spacing**: Prioritize whitespace and breathing room ("double whatever padding you have").
- **Color**: Strict restraint (1 primary accent, 1 neutral scale, clean background, dark crisp text).
- **Components**: Standardize on `shadcn/ui` and accessible primitives (Radix + Tailwind CSS).
- **Competitive Emulation**: Learn from and adapt battle-tested patterns from industry market leaders for the specific domain.
